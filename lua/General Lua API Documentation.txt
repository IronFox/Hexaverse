	bool		Game.AI.IsBase(int);					-- Checks if the specified pole index (1-12) is covered by a Nexus base
	--[[
	General note about the below functions:
	These functions are designed to estimate whether the factories of a nexus base should be activated and a reaction to player presence should be prepared.
	They observe all units, including those not currently visible to the Nexus AI.
	A typical use case is to schedule unit construction until the overall friendly-sum equals or exceeds the respective hostile-sum.
	Ground/air estimation observes units able to attack or scan in those areas. Units are generally classified
	as either ground or air-active, regardless of where they currently are.
	An anti-air tower or mobile unit is air-active, not ground-active.
	Likewise a bomber is considered ground-active, not air-active.
	
	@param pole Pole index in [1,12]
	]]
	AiThreat	Game.AI.GetThreatLevel(int pole);
	AiSupport	Game.AI.GetSupportLevel(int pole);
