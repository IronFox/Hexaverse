# Anti Aircraft

- Cost: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png)10
- [Initiative](../../concepts/initiative.md): 80
- [Weight](../../concepts/vehicle.md): 3
- Base [scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 4
- Sub-orbital [scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 5
- Offensive turret
- Vehicle must remain stationary to fire
- [Base horizontal attack range](../../concepts/attack-range.md): 5 (AAMissile)
- Does not require ammunition
- 1 attack per round
- Explosion radius: 1
- Damage: 10

Sub-orbital planes can be detected and attacked in flight.
The Anti Aircraft turret intercepts hostile mobile units during their movement [phase](../../concepts/round.md) even if itself mounted on a mobile unit. It is thus particularly well suited to establish an ad hoc defense perimeter, or to permanently defend strategically important regions if mounted on a tower.
