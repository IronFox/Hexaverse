# EMP

- Cost: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png)100
- [Initiative](../../concepts/initiative.md): 30
- [Weight](../../concepts/vehicle.md): 5
- Base [scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 3
- Offensive turret
- [Base horizontal attack range](../../concepts/attack-range.md): 15 (Artillery)
- Consumes ![Uranium](../../../../documentation/images/Uranium.png)50 per shot
- 1 attack per round
- Explosion radius: 3
- EMP damage: 5

Units or ground locations can be attacked if in range.
This turret deals EMP damage. EMP damage temporarily reduces attack and [scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md), movement speed, and disallows construction operations. Does not cause any permanent damage.

---

Extremely long range artillery turret, designed to cause [EMP damage](../../concepts/emp-damage.md) in clusters of (hopefully) hostile units.
Doesn't cause permanent damage, and never attacks on its own.
