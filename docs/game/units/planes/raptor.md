# Raptor

- Health: 5
- Price: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png) 120
- [Scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 7
- [Hrz. Attack range](../../concepts/attack-range.md): 9 (Missile)
- Can attack independently of movement action
- Does not require ammunition
- 2 attacks per round
- Explosion radius: 1
- Damage: 5

Units or ground locations can be attacked if in range.

- Can safely fall down 1 height level
- Stationary on ground (supported by water)
- Speed: 10 (aerial)

Sub-orbital planes move above the regular terrain. They can scan the surface, and are invisible to ordinary radar.
