# Anti Aircraft Tower

- Based on [Anti Aircraft](../turrets/anti-aircraft.md) turret
- Health: 10
- Price: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png) 50
- [Scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 2 [=4-2 (tower modifier)]
- [Hrz. Attack range](../../concepts/attack-range.md): 7 [=5+2 (tower modifier)] (AAMissile)
- Sub-orbital [scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 5
- Does not require ammunition
- 1 attack per round
- Explosion radius: 1
- Damage: 10

Sub-orbital planes can be detected and attacked in flight.
The Anti Aircraft tower-turret intercepts hostile mobile units during their movement [phase](../../concepts/round.md) even if itself mounted on a mobile unit. It is thus particularly well suited to establish an ad hoc defense perimeter, or to permanently defend strategically important regions if mounted on a tower.
