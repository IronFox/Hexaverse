# Mine

- Health: 8
- Price: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png) 40
- [Scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 2
- Consumes: ![Energy](../../../../documentation/images/Energy.png)10

Enabled mines extract any natural [resources](../../concepts/resources.md) available in the planet-core beneath their location. They are essential in supplying other structures of the local [compound](../../concepts/compound.md). Mines should be build only on known occurrences of natural [resources](../../concepts/resources.md). They cannot be constructed atop each other, since they require a working link to the planet core in order to operate. Mines, like many structures, require ![Energy](../../../../documentation/images/Energy.png) from [generator](generator.md)s to operate.
