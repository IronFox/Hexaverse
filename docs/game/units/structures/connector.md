# Connector

- Health: 1
- Price: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png) 5
- [Scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 2

Connectors establish links between structures that are too distant from one another to otherwise connect. Connected structures form [compound](../../concepts/compound.md)s and share their input/output.
