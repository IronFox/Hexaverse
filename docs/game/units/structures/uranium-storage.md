# Uranium Storage

- Health: 2
- Price: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png) 10
- [Scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 2
- Storage capacity: ![Uranium](../../../../documentation/images/Uranium.png)500

Storage structures accumulate excess production of the respective [resources](../../concepts/resources.md) from the local [compound](../../concepts/compound.md) until they are full. Disabling a storage structure will change its priority such, that excess [resources](../../concepts/resources.md) are stored here only if all other storage capacities are exhausted. Likewise, resource demand will remove [resources](../../concepts/resources.md) from disabled storage structures first, such that they tend towards empty. Storage structures do not require ![Energy](../../../../documentation/images/Energy.png) to operate.
