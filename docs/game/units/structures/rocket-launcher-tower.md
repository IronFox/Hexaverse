# Rocket Launcher Tower

- Based on [Rocket Launcher](../turrets/rocket-launcher.md) turret
- Health: 22 [=11*2 (turret health multiplier)]
- Price: ![Minerals](../../../../documentation/images/Minerals.png) 55
- [Scanning range](../../concepts/scanning-range.md): 3 [=5-2 (tower modifier)]
- [Hrz. Attack range](../../concepts/attack-range.md): 6 [=4+2 (tower modifier)] (Missile)
- Does not require ammunition
- 2 attacks per round
- Explosion radius: 1
- Damage: 7

Units or ground locations can be attacked if in range.
The Rocket Launcher tower-turret intercepts hostile mobile units during their movement [phase](../../concepts/round.md). It is thus particularly well suited to defend strategically important regions.

---

Cheap, low range defense tower, capable of firing twice per round.
Good enough against hostile units using [canon](../turrets/cannon.md) turrets. Insufficient when facing [artillery](../turrets/artillery.md).
Fires in a straight line.
