# EMP Damage

EMP damage is caused by [EMP turrets](../units/turrets/emp.md) or [residual EMP zones](../terrain.md).

Units affected by EMP damage are considerably slowed down (if mobile), have extremely reduced attack and scanning ranges, and cannot perform operations (e.g. [terraforming](../units/turrets/terraformer.md)).

The effect is worse the higher the level of existing EMP damage, but shows the biggest difference between 0 (no damage), 1, and 2.

EMP damage does not stack, and heals by one each [round](round.md).
