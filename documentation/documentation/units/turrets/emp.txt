----
Extremely long range artillery turret, designed to cause EMP damage in clusters of (hopefully) hostile units.
Units affected by EMP damage are considerably slowed down (if mobile), have extremely reduced attack and scanning ranges, and cannot perform operations (e.g. [[units/turrets/terraformer|terraform]]).
EMP damage heals by one each [[concepts/round]].

Doesn't cause permanent damage, and never fires on its own.
