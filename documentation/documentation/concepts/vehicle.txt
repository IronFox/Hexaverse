= Vehicle =

Vehicles are ground-based mobile units built by [[units/structures/vehicle plant]]s. They require at least a ledge to stand on, and cannot stand on lava alone.
All regular vehicles are composed of a vehicle base and turret.
Depending on the combination, different capacities and restrictions apply.

== Weight ==

Turrets come with an inherent weight, which must be carried by the respective vehicle base.
Overweight is the result of turret weight exceeding the carrying capacity of the used base.

For each additional point of overweight, the vehicle will lose 25% of its remaining movement speed, though it will never fall below one step per [[concepts/round]].
Heavy turrets, such as a [[units/turrets/bomb]] or [[units/turrets/missile launcher]], are thus better suited atop a [[units/vbases/heavy base|heavy]] or [[units/vbases/catamaran base]].


== Ships ==
Vehicles built on a [[units/vbases/catamaran base]] are much faster on, but also restricted to water surfaces. They are usually a good choice on planets with extensive water surfaces, but rarely anywhere else.
Naval [[units/turrets/terraformer]]s can dig canals through non-water terrain, but can never leave the water surface.

==Heavy Vehicles==
If a [[units/vbases/heavy base]] is used, then the vehicle cannot savely stand on water alone, and has reduced falling tolerance. However, similar to catcases it does come along with increased capacities on solid terrain. Heavy vehicles benefit from flat non-liquid terrain.
Similarly to naval vehicles, [[units/turrets/terraformer]]s can make any terrain suitable for heavy vehicles.
