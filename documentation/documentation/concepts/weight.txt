= Weight =

Turrets come with an inherent weight, which must be carried by the respective vehicle base.
Overweight is the result of turret weight exceeding the carrying capacity of the used base.

For each additional point of overweight, the vehicle will lose 25% of its remaining movement speed, though it will never fall below one step per [[concepts/round]].
Heavy turrets, such as a [[units/turrets/bomb]] or [[units/turrets/missile launcher]], are thus better suited atop a [[units/vbases/heavy base|heavy]] or [[units/vbases/catamaran base]].
