= Faction = 

Each connected player client allows control of one faction. All units and their [[concepts/scanning range|sensory]] are shared among each other and the player client.
Structures of the same faction implicitly connect into [[concepts/compound|compounds]] to share [[concepts/resources]].
Auto-firing towers will use the sensory of any other unit of the same faction to extend their own vision.

Players are given control of a new faction during their first connection.
A secret key is transferred that allows the client to resume control of this faction later on.
Upon reconnection, players may choose to resume their faction or create a new one.
However, only one key per planet ID is locally preserved.
