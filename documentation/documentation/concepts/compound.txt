=Compound=

Neighboring structures of at most one height layer of difference form compounds.
Within a compound, working structures auto-balance [[concepts/resources|resource]] output and demand. Passive structures may function as relays between distant structures.
Select any structure of a compound to see its resource balance in the bottom left screen corner.

In some game variances all structures of one [[concepts/faction]] form an implicit compound, regardless of distance.
In others, [[units/structures/connector|connectors]] are required to connect distant structures.


==Structure Enabled State==

Many structure types can be enabled or disabled.
Depending on the state of their compound, it may not be possible to enable a particular structure at a time without first disabling some other structure.
In particular, if a structure consumes limited resources, it may disallow other structures in the same compound to be enabled.

The game tries to balance supply and demand automatically.
If, for instance, insufficient energy is available to explicitly enable a certain structure, but [[units/structures/generator|generators]] could be enabled, then it will do so automatically.
This, in turn, might enable fuel [[units/structures/mine|mines]] that have not been running at this point.

Rapid change (such as [[units/structures/connector|connectors]] being destroyed) will see the minimal number of structures disabled to balance out [[concepts/resources|resource]] supply and demand.
