additional vehicle state 'virtual'
	not part of the physical reality of the planet
	block each other. avoid byt are not blocked by rw vehicles
	are blocked by rw buildings and walls
	can move at increased pace, with special computation phases executed during regular command phase
	can see each other and everything within the visible world of the access node they connected to
	are invisible to regular factions
	
	
additional building type 'access node'
	broadcasted to nearby systems
	players can dispatch 'virtual' vehicles into the visible world space of the owning faction
	function as spawn points for these vehicles
	large numbers of virtual vehicles require more access nodes

	
interactive building types accessible by virtual vehicles
	


planet leases
	interactive buildings allow leasing a predefined area within the domain of another faction
	allows free alteration of that area, including the creation of space for virtual vehicles and interactive structures
